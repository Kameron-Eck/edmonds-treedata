**Date:** 2026-09-08. **Brief:** `Reports/LITREVIEW_HEALING_ANALOGUES_PROMPT_2026-09-08.md` (the block under "## The prompt"). **Companion table:** `Reports/lit_healing_analogues_2026-09-08.csv` (one row per mechanism, 72 rows). **Method:** ten field-finder agents (one per field), twelve adversarial skeptics (one per group of recommended mechanisms, three lenses each, every cited page re-opened), one completeness critic, one orchestrator. ## 0. Summary

**What was asked.** Find, in ten fields outside canopy mapping, mechanisms that correct a per-time-step classification by borrowing from neighbouring time steps, under a one-sided error model, with a bounded and auditable licence to change a label — and, for each, the evidence that licenses a correction, whether it is one-directional, the stated bound, how real change survives, how it was validated, and what can be borrowed verbatim.

**What was found, in one paragraph.** The ten fields supply the *shape* of the project's bounded backfill many times over — bracketing, gap caps, asymmetric birth/death, threshold relaxation under temporal support, marker-under-mask, extensive closings, covariate-indexed hangovers, existence posteriors with a separate survival term — and supply **no measured laundering rate for any of them**. The thing the brief asked for — a one-directional temporal correction with a *measured* false-correction rate on real gold — was hunted in all ten fields and found in none (§5, §8). Of 72 mechanisms returned, 26 were labelled RECOMMEND by the finders; after adversarial re-reading of every cited page, **one stands** (an error-attribution audit, as a measurement instrument), **seventeen are demoted to LEAD**, and **eight are refuted as proposed** (§9). The refutations are mostly not misreadings of papers — the finders' quotations were almost all verbatim-accurate — but failures of *transposition*: gates that need two anchors were built on mechanisms that fire from one or none; a Bayesian fill licence that cannot refuse at the project's measured rates; an error-model asymmetry borrowed from a field whose asymmetry runs the other way; a one-directionality guarantee that bounds the direction the project already forbids.

**Four findings that cut across fields (§3, §6):**
1. **None of the probabilistic mechanisms recommended requires canopy on both sides of a gap.** Existence filters, occupancy posteriors, depth-weighted likelihoods, PU imputation, hysteresis, reconstruction, the P.56 hangover, Ramírez's hangover, Schmidt's update table and Vijayanarasimhan-Grauman propagation all fill from one anchor or zero. The two-sided constructions that exist (trajectory interpolation, set-nesting, the 1-D morphological closing, the HMM bridging inequality as stated) are non-probabilistic or unmeasured and carry no laundering rate. The project's both-sides rule is external to every field.
2. **Four fields converge on one formula** — fill odds = (1−ε)^(k+1)·∏(1−p_t) / [ε·γ·(1−γ)^(k−1)] — and at the project's measured rates it cannot refuse a fill. The denominator needs **P(regain | recent removal)**; the panel's 2 verified gains supply P(colonisation | empty site) — 2×10⁻⁴ to 6×10⁻⁴ per year depending on the denominator (§3) — two to three orders of magnitude too small and the wrong quantity, and the ~3-year coppice window is currently the only estimate of the conditional rate. The posterior is inert for want of one number the project does not have (γ_conditional), not because posteriors cannot discriminate.
3. **The "formally one-directional operator" guarantee points the wrong way.** Extensivity bounds 1→0, which the project already forbids by construction, and bounds nothing on 0→1, the primary failure. A bound on the permitted direction requires a laundering measurement. No field has one.
4. **The 42-loss ceiling.** Zero laundered of 42 verified losses bounds the loss-side laundering rate at 6.9% (95%, exact), not zero — and only if all 42 were at risk: for a both-sides heal a verified loss can be filled only if its sequence carries a detection *after* the loss (n_eligible, §4.9 M7), so the at-risk denominator must be reported with the count, and if it is small the "zero laundered" criterion is near-vacuous for both-sides rules rather than a 6.9% bound. Certifying below 1% on the loss side needs ~300 verified losses (~8,650 labelled points at 3.5% prevalence). A **fill-side** audit — ~300 cells the heal changed, sampled from the heal's own output — fits in one session and can bound the fill-side rate below 1%. Both denominators must be published.

**Three to test first (§7):** (1) the fill-side laundering audit with a null-change control; (2) the gap-length spectrum H(L) with laundered(L), triples(L), n_eligible and crowns-deleted columns, L in years, mutation-tested, zero labels; (3) measure γ_conditional from the adjudicated impossible triples at the verified no-change points (the only population in which a loss-then-regain can appear), and crown-level α plus the post-loss false-canopy rate from the 42 verified-loss trajectories. **One to refuse, until γ_conditional has been measured:** any posterior-as-licence (PMBM existence, dynamic-occupancy, SAR-EM imputation) — single- or zero-anchor, inert at the available γ, and a calibrated-looking number that invites a lower review threshold.

## 1. Method

**Phase 1 — fan-out.** Ten Opus finders, one per field, each with the full brief; each required to return ≥5 mechanisms in a fixed template (citation with fetch status, the six answers each tagged measured/assumed/inferred, the paper's own false-correction rate verbatim with location, two counter-arguments, a LABEL of RECOMMEND / LEAD / REJECT, and a pre-filled CSV row), plus a field-level assumption-fit score, a NOT FOUND ledger and a pages-fetched ledger. A citation without a fetched page was disallowed; a fetch failure was recorded as UNREADABLE.

**Phase 2 — adversarial verification.** Every RECOMMEND (26) was assigned to a skeptic (12 skeptics, grouped by shared source) who re-opened the cited pages and tried to refute the finder's reading under three lenses: **assumption mismatch** (one-sided error? covariate-dependent miss? what do we violate?), **laundering** (could it erase real change; does the paper report a true-transition-destroyed rate; and, for probabilistic mechanisms, the **single-anchor probe**: does it need presence on both sides or does one presence plus low-detectability absences propagate?), and **validation feasibility at 250–2,000 labels** (with arithmetic). Measurement mechanisms were instead asked whether the metric could *hide* a laundering event. Each mechanism received an evidence-basis class — MEASURED-RATE / FORMAL-BOUND / STRUCTURAL-OR-IDENTIFIABILITY-ONLY / DESIGN-ONLY / SECONDARY-SOURCE-ONLY — under the brief's rule that a method that smooths without a laundering analysis is a lead, not a recommendation, with an exemption only for pure measurement instruments and formally one-directional operators. Verdicts: STANDS / WEAKENED (→ LEAD) / REFUTED (dropped, refutation kept).

**Phase 3 — synthesis (orchestrator).** Ranking by the brief's four assumption axes; the canonical-formula convergence checked algebraically; borrowable gates restated with the skeptics' fixes; the not-found list assembled per field.

**Phase 4 — completeness critic.** One Opus agent read the draft and named what was missing; candidates it raised were verified by fetch before being acted on (§9).

**Reading the verdict labels.** *Finder label* is the finder's original call. *Skeptic verdict* is after re-fetch: **STANDS** (could not refute), **WEAKENED** (demoted to LEAD; the specific defect is stated), **REFUTED** (the mechanism as proposed fails one of the project's stated constraints; what survives, if anything, is named). Mechanisms the finders labelled LEAD or REJECT were not adjudicated and carry the finder's label in the CSV.

## 3. The one formula four fields converge on — and why it cannot license a bracketed fill

**A note on the panel counts, once.** The brief states 42 verified losses and 1,170 verified no-change points; the frozen change gold records 1,169 no-change + 42 losses + 2 gains = 1,213 (`Reports/CHANGE_DETECTOR_DESIGN_2026-09-06.md`, which names the 1,155 / 1,169 / 1,170 drift). This report uses the brief's counts (1,170; 1,212) throughout, except in this section where the gold's 2 gains are the subject; the one-point difference changes no number to two significant figures.

**The object.** A crown is observed PRESENT at t₀, ABSENT at k acquisitions t₁…t_k, PRESENT at t_{k+1}. Let p_t be the detection probability at acquisition t for that crown (read from the measured recall curve at its size, that year's GSD and season; miss m_t = 1 − p_t), the false-alarm rate ≈ 0, ε the per-interval loss probability, γ the per-interval regain probability. Two dominant latent paths:

- FILL (present throughout; all k zeros are misses): L_fill = (1−ε)^(k+1) · ∏_{t∈gap} (1 − p_t)
- LOSS-THEN-REGAIN (removed after t₀, absent, re-established by t_{k+1}): L_loss = ε · (1−γ)^(k−1) · γ

**Fill odds Λ = L_fill / L_loss.** The exact posterior sums over every latent path (forward-backward); the two-path form is the dominant-path approximation and, for k > 1, an approximation that degrades with p — at k = 2 the exact change-path mass is (1−ε)εγ(1−p) + εγ(1−ε)(1−p) + ε(1−γ)γ, summing over the latent gap states (1,0), (0,1), (0,0), against the two-path form [1 − (1−ε)²]·γ; at ε = 0.0044, γ = 2.1×10⁻⁴, p = 0.9 that is 1.09×10⁻⁶ against 1.81×10⁻⁶, the F8 skeptic's ratio of 0.60 (reproduced by the orchestrator). ε and γ must be per acquisition-interval, 1 − (1−ε_annual)^Δt, not per acquisition, because the archive is irregular (F9b skeptic).

**Who states it.** Field 8 — MacKenzie et al. 2003, Eqs. 3–5 with K=1 give Pr(occupied throughout | 1,0,1) = (1−ε)²(1−p) / [(1−ε)²(1−p) + εγ], which is Λ/(1+Λ) at k=1; the skeptic re-derived it and confirmed no latent path is omitted [inferred, verified]. Field 4 — the finder's bridging inequality from a two-state HMM with the false-alarm emission fixed at zero: fill iff ∏m_t·p₁₁^(k+1) > p₁₀·p₀₁·p₀₀^(k−1), the same object with p₁₁ = 1−ε, p₁₀ = ε, p₀₁ = γ [inferred]. Field 3 — Gu, Ma & Balasubramanian 2015 Eq. 2.1 normalised over candidate removal intervals with φ₁ = 1: the numerator is identical; the denominator is *absent* because the model is permanent-event (γ ≡ 0, Λ = ∞), which is the algebraic reason a coppice return is a model violation there [inferred from the fetched equation]. Field 7 — the finder's depth-weighted gate Σ_y −log(1−p_y) ≥ τ is ∏m_t ≤ e^{−τ}: the same left-hand side against a fixed α instead of the prior odds εγ; the skeptic established this object is Kasliwal et al. 2020's joint non-detection probability ("our joint probability of detecting zero kilonovae is only 4.2%"), reported but never thresholded, not Mohite's Eq. 14 (a selection density inside a population likelihood).

**Why it is inert here.** On a *trailing* run (present then all-zero to the end) loss enters as **+ε alone** — MacKenzie 2003 Eq. 4, p.2202: "Pr(X_{i,2} = 000 | X_{i,1}) = (1 − ε_1) ∏_{j=1}^{3} (1 − p_{2,j}) + ε_1" — first-order and competitive with the miss product. On a *bracketed* gap loss enters as the **product εγ**. With the panel's rates — the frozen change gold is 1,169 no-change / 42 loss / 2 gain over 2016→2024 (`Reports/CHANGE_DETECTOR_DESIGN_2026-09-06.md`, "arbitrated by re-deduplicating the label+verify logs"; the brief's 1,170 is the count drift that document names) — ε = 42/1,213 over 8 years → 0.0044/yr; γ from the 2 gains → 2.1×10⁻⁴/yr on the all-points denominator the F8 skeptic used, or ≈ 5.9×10⁻⁴/yr on an empty-site denominator of ~422 points (the 2 gains plus the "420 of 1169 no-change points [that] can't produce a false loss at all" which the same document records [inferred: those are the points with no canopy at 2016]) — the F8 skeptic computed Pr(no change | 1,0,1) = 0.999999 at p = 0.3, 0.999991 at p = 0.9, 0.999908 at p = 0.99; to fall below 0.99 needs p > 0.99991; below 0.5 needs p > 0.9999991; at the 95% upper bound on γ still p > 0.99967. On the empty-site denominator the refusal threshold is p > 0.9997; even at γ = 0.02 it is p > 0.991. The conclusion does not depend on which denominator is right, because both measure colonisation of an empty site, not regain after a removal. **Any pre-registered threshold returns HEAL for every acquisition.** The sensitivity table shows where the formula *does* work: at γ ≈ 0.2 the posterior drops below 0.99 for p > 0.912 — fine-year absences refuse, coarse-year absences fill, which is the project's tier logic reproduced. So the formula is not incapable; it is starved. **The denominator needs P(regain | recent removal) — a conditional coppice/replant rate — and the panel supplies P(colonisation | empty site), three orders of magnitude smaller; the ~3-year regrowth window the project already uses is currently the only estimate of the conditional rate.** This is also why the project's *hard* gates (gap length in years, size floor, lidar veto) are doing work: they encode γ_conditional as a rule because it has not been measured.

**Two further caveats every field shares.** (a) Independence of misses across acquisitions is assumed by all four forms and is false (recall–canopy-fraction r = 0.91); under positive correlation ∏(1−p_t) under-states P(all missed | present), so the formulas err toward *declaring* loss — fabrication at the 1,170 no-change points, the mirror failure, on a population 28× larger than the losses (F7 skeptic). (b) On the ~33 acquisitions outside the verified interval, p_t measured against projected-2020 labels is circular (a real removal is scored as a miss), biasing p_t downward and the gate toward *filling* (F7 skeptic's worked case, with τ = −ln 0.05 = 2.996: two absences at true ε = 0.80 give Σ −ln(1−ε) = 3.22 > τ, "declare loss"; measured as 0.73 they give 2.62 < τ, "fill"). Two unbounded biases of opposite sign.

**What to keep.** The formula as an **audit statistic** (report Λ per healed gap next to the tier that licensed it) and as the definition of the missing measurement (γ_conditional). Not as a licence — see §7.

### 4.1 Field 1 — Multi-object tracking and object permanence (fit 5/8; rank 5)

**The brief's question — what gap lengths are bridged, on what evidence, and how do trackers avoid stitching two objects?** Gap lengths: SORT T_Lost = 1 frame; DeepSORT A_max = 30 frames; ByteTrack lost-track buffer 30 frames; StrongSORT GSI "the maximum gap allowed for interpolation is 20 frames" — every value is ~0.03–1 s of video, tuned on a dense-gold validation split, derived from no bound [measured]. Evidence: geometric/appearance association either side of the gap (IoU, Mahalanobis, cosine); during the gap nothing licenses anything — the track is simply not yet deleted. Stitching is avoided by the cap and by spatial gates (AFLink: 30 frames, 75 px), never by a probabilistic test; and **the standard benchmarks do not evaluate departure-and-return at all** — MOT16 §IV-A1: "when a target leaves the field-of-view and then reappears, it is treated as an unseen target with a new ID" [measured]. The field has no measured false-stitch rate on real gold in the project's gap regime; on *simulated* gold the u-track paper (Jaqaman et al. 2008, Nature Methods, PMC2747604 — opened by the completeness critic) reports "there were only 10% false links" at its highest particle density, with gap closing over "the time window of eight frames chosen for gap closing", against "simulated trajectories (ground truth; GT)" — a false-stitch rate at a stated gap length, on synthetic trajectories of moving particles [measured]. The re-identification literature proper (appearance matching across unbounded gaps on human-annotated identities), the brief's named sub-topic and the one place field 1 has real gold for the stitching question, was not reached by the finder and is recorded as a gap in §8. The one field-level number, 781 identity switches / 830 ground-truth test tracks ≈ 0.94 per trajectory (DeepSORT on MOT16), is an upper envelope on identity error of either kind [inferred arithmetic on two fetched numbers].

### 4.3 Field 3 — Interval-censored survival, current-status data, monotone regression, change-points (fit 6/8; rank 3)

**The brief's question — is there a standard estimator for a crown's validity interval?** Yes, two, and neither is plain Turnbull. For *repeated visits per unit*: the sequential-imperfect-test mixture likelihood (Balasubramanian & Lagakos 2003 → Gu, Ma & Balasubramanian 2015, R `icensmis`), which sums over candidate event intervals with the per-visit reading probabilities as factors. Two limits: every implementation readable takes a *scalar* (φ₁, φ₀) — visit-varying sensitivity is structurally free but not implemented or validated anywhere readable; and accuracy is *not identifiable* from the sequence alone — "one cannot estimate the test accuracy parameters accurately without the validation data" (Chen & Wang 2026) — so the 250–2,000-point session *is* the validation subsample the theory demands.

### 4.7 Field 7 — Astronomy and transient detection (fit 4/8; rank 7)

**The brief's question — how is completeness used to weight an absence?** In two ways, and the field practises the second without writing it down. (1) *Forced photometry* refuses to let a non-detection become a zero: at every epoch the flux is re-measured at the known position with a fixed PSF model and reported as a signed value with an error bar; a non-detection is an upper limit "mag = zpdiff − 2.5 log10(SNU × forcediffimfluxunc)" with recommended SNT = 3, SNU = 5 (Masci et al. 2023); Rubin mandates it for every DIAObject in the field of view (LSE-163) [measured]. A deep epoch's silence is a strong absence (small upper limit); a shallow epoch's silence is uninformative (large upper limit). (2) *Population likelihoods* multiply a term per non-detection whose value depends on that epoch's limiting magnitude (Mohite et al. 2021 Eqs. 8, 14) and report the joint miss probability (Kasliwal et al. 2020: "our joint probability of detecting zero kilonovae is only 4.2%") — reported, never thresholded, never applied to a single object [measured]. No fetched paper states in prose that a deeper non-detection outweighs a shallower one; Carbone et al. 2016 depth-weight inside a Monte Carlo (the finder's quotation "no explicit equation differentiates contributions by sensitivity" does not appear in Carbone and was a summariser's report — dropped). Completeness itself is measured by injection-recovery of synthetic sources (Kessler 2015: "ϵS/N is empirically measured from the fakes"), so every completeness curve in the field is validated on synthetic gold — UNVALIDATED against real sources in the project's sense. Two disciplines transfer verbatim: index the curve by the *injected* (true) property, never the recovered one (a wrong choice costs "0.2 dex" at M_in = −22, MNRAS 509:5836), and pass every verification point through the *whole* pipeline including the gate (the DES design).

### 4.8 Field 8 — Ecology and occupancy modelling (fit 7/8; rank 1)

**The brief's question — how is detection separated from occupancy when both vary with covariates, and what happens with one visit per season?** Separation comes from *replication within closure*: with T repeat visits inside a window during which the true state cannot change, the all-zero history factorises as ψ∏(1−p_t) + (1−ψ) and p is estimable as a covariate function (MacKenzie 2002 Eq. 2 logistic link) — "unbiased for T ≥ 5 when p ≥ 0.3"; "caution if ψ near 1 when p < 0.15 and occasions < 7" [measured, simulation]. With **one visit per season the model is not identified from the data alone**: MacKenzie 2003 p.2206, "If all sites are surveyed only once per primary period, then detection probability will be confounded with the parameters of interest, resulting in estimates that are biased to an unknown degree and unknown direction" [measured]. Three escapes: (i) a numeric covariate acting on p but not on ψ (Lele, Moreno & Bayne 2012 — contested by Knape & Korner-Nievergelt 2015, unreadable, reported via Solymos & Lele's abstract), with a data-cloning estimability test that costs zero labels; (ii) calibrate p externally on a replicated sub-sample and plug it in (MacKenzie 2002 p.2253; 2003 p.2206) — a Discussion-section design neither paper validates, and the replicate must be a conditionally independent draw from a *common* detection process, which a human re-reading the same pixels is not; (iii) a structurally-certain detection channel (Miller 2011) pins the false-positive rate — earned there by dip-netting frogs, a different instrument from re-reading imagery. Sample sizes the field states: 100–200 sites for means, ≥ 300 for regression coefficients (Lele p.25); cumulative detection 1 − ∏(1−p̂_t) in [0.85, 0.95] as the design target (Guillera-Arroita 2014). The dynamic model's posterior on a bracketed gap (§3) is the field's algebraic statement of the BLIND tier: (1−p) and εγ trade off one-for-one.

**The brief's question — cheap baselines to compare against.** Three, in order of what they cost and what they guarantee. ## 6. Borrowable gates and statistics that survived, with the skeptics' fixes attached

Each entry: the exact formula or procedure, the ground truth it needs, feasibility at 250–2,000 labels per session, and the source. Entries marked *instrument* write no label.

**G1. Fill-side laundering audit, output-sampled** (F10 M3 transposed + F7 M4's validation architecture; *instrument*). Procedure: (i) run the heal; (ii) take the set C of crown-years the heal changed 0→1; (iii) draw n cells from C at random (stratified by tier and by acquisition class if the count allows); (iv) a human adjudicates each in the *interval* form — was the crown present in that year, given the pair of neighbouring years (paired read, not blinded chip); (iv-b) check the adjudicator against an independent modality — a lidar epoch, finer imagery — on a sub-sample of the same cells, because a human reading the same pixels the model read is not independent of the model, and without this step k/n bounds human–model agreement, not laundering (the defect the skeptics used against F8 M3; the remedy is G11's); (v) report k laundered of n with the exact one-sided 95% upper bound 1 − 0.05^(1/n) when k = 0 (Clopper–Pearson otherwise); (vi) publish it *next to* the loss-side count (k′ of 42) and its bound. Both halves are required: panel-only scoring can be gamed by a heal that never touches labelled points; output-only scoring loses the count on the verified losses. Ground truth: ~300 adjudicated fill cells for a < 1% bound. Feasible: yes, one session. Teeth: the base loss rate is 3.47%, so a fill-side rate below 1% requires the heal to fill real-loss cells at under 29% of the rate it fills random cells. Source: Kola 2011 (name and idea; the 0.0016 does not transfer); Kessler 2019 (pass every verification point through the whole pipeline).

**G2. Report the loss-side ceiling, never "zero"** (F1 M5, F2 M2, F2 M7, F3 M5, F6 M3, F9 M3/M7, F10 M3 — arrived at independently by seven mechanisms; *instrument*). 0 of 42 → 6.9% (exact, 1 − 0.05^(1/42)) or 7.1% (rule of three) — and only over the at-risk set: for a both-sides heal a loss can be filled only if a detection follows it, so report n_eligible (§4.9 M7) beside the count; with n_eligible = 5 the exact bound is 45%, and with n_eligible = 0 the criterion cannot fire and certifies nothing about both-sides rules. 5% needs ~59 losses (~1,700 points at 3.5% prevalence, one session); 1% needs ~299 losses (~8,650 points). Compare candidate rules on the losses *paired* (McNemar on discordant losses), which is materially more powerful than comparing absolute rates. Ground truth: the existing 42. Feasible: the number is free; tightening it is not.

**G3. The gap-length spectrum** (F9 M7 → M3; *instrument*). H(L) = Σ φ_L(x) − Σ x over the x-y-t stack, for a nested convex family of 1-D structuring elements; report alongside laundered(L) on the 42 losses, triples(L) on the 1,170 no-change points, **n_eligible** (the number of the 42 whose sequence carries any post-loss 1 — if zero, laundered(L) is structurally pinned at zero and the instrument has no brake), and **crowns_deleted(L)** if any floor is applied. Fixes: L is specified in *years since last observation* (1 − (1−ε_annual)^Δt for any rate that enters), not in acquisitions; the selection rule is "largest L whose 95% upper bound on the laundering rate is below a pre-set tolerance", not L\* = max{L : laundered(L) = 0} (an argmax over the region where the test has no power); mutation-test laundered(·) by seeding a known-bad L before trusting it; monotonicity holds only if the licence set does not depend on L and the SE family stays nested and convex. Ground truth: none to compute; the panel to overlay. Feasible: yes, zero labels. Source: Breen, Jones & Talbot 2000 eq. (3) and the absorption law, by duality to closings (sanctioned on the same page).

**G4. The 1-D closing as comparator baseline, and extensivity as a write-time assertion** (F9 M3, F10 M1). Baseline: x • B_L = (x ⊕ B_L) ⊖ B_L along t; fills interior 0-runs of length ≤ L−1 flanked by 1s [derived, confirmed]; padding convention must be stated (zero-padding leaves terminal runs unfilled; reflect-padding does not). Assertion: for whatever heal is adopted, assert heal(x) ≥ x pixel-wise before writing (extensivity), and assert heal(heal(x)) = heal(x) on a sample (idempotence — can fail if a size floor is evaluated on the current mask's components). Ground truth: none. Feasible: yes. Source: Breen 2000 Table 1, §3.2; Hlaváč slides 35–38.

**G5. The size floor as an anti-extensive guard on the added set only** (F9 M4). Output = x ∪ γ_λ(φ_L(x) \ x), where γ_λ is the area opening with an increasing criterion and λ(year) is the crown area at which that year's measured recall crosses the chosen floor. Never apply γ_λ to the whole map (deletes correctly detected small crowns: 1→0). Publish TP/FN/FP before and after per acquisition *with a crowns-deleted column*. Ground truth: the recall-vs-crown-size curve (held for 2016–2024; extrapolated elsewhere). Feasible: yes for the operator; the audit table needs object-level gold that a point panel does not supply (FP and PWC are not computable from points). Source: Breen 2000 §3.6.

**G6. The temporal median as negative control** (F9 M6). Run the median heal with a matched window; report its laundered-loss count beside the adopted heal's. Free. Source: self-duality of the median on {0,1} [derived]; Hlaváč slide 38 for the opening/closing duality it contrasts with.

**G7. The fill-odds statistic as an audit, and the definition of the missing measurement** (F8 M2 / F4 / F3 M2 / F7 M3; *instrument*). Per healed gap of k acquisitions spanning Δt years: Λ = (1−ε_Δ)^(k+1)·∏_{t∈gap}(1−p_t) / [ε_Δ·γ_Δ·(1−γ_Δ)^(k−1)], with ε_Δ = 1 − (1−ε_annual)^Δt and γ_Δ likewise, p_t from the recall curve at the crown's *2020* size (never the size measured in the gap year). Report Λ next to the tier that licensed the fill. Under positive miss correlation report also the conservative max_t(1−p_t) form. **γ_conditional** — P(a removed crown's footprint reads present again within Δt) — is the number this statistic needs and the project does not have; the panel's 2 gains give P(colonisation | empty site), ~3 orders too small. Ground truth for γ_conditional: NOT the 42 verified losses — they are endpoint-verified absent at 2024, so a crown removed and regrown inside the interval reads present–present and sits in the no-change set; conditioning on the 42 conditions on no regain (the critic's correction of this report's first draft). The population that can contain a loss-then-regain is the impossible triples (present–absent–present) and interior absent runs at the verified no-change points, adjudicated as detection failure versus bare ground / stump / replant; the 42 yield α and the post-loss false-canopy / ingrowth rate (see §7). Feasible: yes — interior reads of an existing panel. Sources: MacKenzie 2003 Eqs. 3–5 (posterior re-derived); Gu 2015 Eq. 2.1; the F4 bridging inequality.

**G8. Weight an absence by completeness; never impute** (F5 M3/M4, F7 M2/M3). With e(x) = Pr(detected | present, x) = the measured recall at (crown size, effective GSD, season), and f(x) the model's prior canopy probability: Pr(present | absent reading, x) = f(x)(1−e(x)) / (1 − f(x)e(x)) (Elkan & Noto Eq. 3 form; Bekker 2019 E-step form). Use it as a *weight on evidence inside the both-sides rule* — it has zero anchors on its own and, as an imputation, fills real removals from prior appearance. Ground truth: e(x) for panel-covered years is measured; for the other ~33 acquisitions e measured against projected-2020 labels is circular and biased low. Feasible: yes where the panel covers. Also keep the F7 kernel: keep the continuous per-year raster and record an absence as a bounded quantity, not a zero — but not with σ from within-crown variance and not with SNT = 3 / SNU = 5.

**G9. Per-year implied-threshold diagnostic** (F5 M2; *instrument*). θ_implied(year) = (1/2 − ρ₋₁) / (1 − ρ₊₁ − ρ₋₁) with ρ₊₁ = 1 − recall and ρ₋₁ = the false-canopy rate *of the projected training labels* for that year (growth), both measured. Report as a bias statistic; do not shift the cut until ρ₋₁ has been measured in at least two early years, because the sign of the shift depends on which of ρ₊₁, ρ₋₁ is larger. Ground truth: ρ₊₁ to ±0.05 needs n ≈ 246–384 verified canopy points per stratum; ρ₋₁ needs verified background points in early years. Feasible: yes for a few strata; not for (acquisition × size). Source: Natarajan 2013 Lemma 7.

**G10. Population debias in F-space** (F3 M1; *instrument*). F̂(t) = (Ĝ(t) − 1 + β_t) / (α + β_t − 1), where Ĝ(t) is the fraction of the fixed 2020 crown set read ABSENT at t, β_t the recall of that acquisition's class, α the crown-level P(a truly removed footprint reads absent at ≥ 0.5). Apply per year *without* PAVA in G-space (G is non-monotone whenever β rises over time — the archive's orientation); if a monotone curve is wanted, pool in F-space with per-year weights (de Leeuw, Hornik & Mair 2009). Use as an audit: does the healed series' implied cumulative loss agree with the misclassification-corrected estimate? Ground truth: β_t to ±0.05 needs n ≳ 250 per class; α from the 42 losses (binomial SE ≈ 0.046 if α ≈ 0.9, ≈ 0.03 if α ≈ 0.95 — 42 events bound α only to roughly ±0.06–0.09, and the value ≈ 1 currently in use is assumed, not measured). Feasible: yes for ~5 classes (~1,250 labels), one interval. Source: McKeown & Jewell 2010.

**G11. Per-year false-canopy audit and the accumulation bound** (F8 M5/M6; *instrument*). Sample n model-asserted canopy cells per stratum, verify, estimate p10 with a Clopper–Pearson interval; check the verifier against an independent modality (lidar epoch, finer imagery) on a sub-sample — a human re-reading the same pixels is not a structural zero. Size: ±50% relative needs n ≈ 130; ±25% needs ≈ 518; 2,000 labels buys ~4 strata, not 40. Then the Royle & Link bound P(spurious persistence) = 1 − (1 − p10)^T with T = the number of *post-gap* acquisitions, not 36. Sources: Miller 2011 Table 1; Royle & Link 2006 p.839.

**G12. Label-targeting sets** (F4 M4, F2 M1 Step 5, F5 M7; *instruments*). (i) Run both HMM decoders (posterior marginal with a cut; Viterbi MAP) and label only the units where they disagree — the laundering-risk population; (ii) allocate the next labelling tranche by expected error (V&G Eq. 13, with the constants re-set); (iii) fit Q̂_t continuous vs discontinuous per year and read the gap as a measure of how idiosyncratic a year's miss rate is. All zero-label to compute. Sources: Rabiner 1989 Eqs. 26–29; Vijayanarasimhan & Grauman 2012 Eqs. 11–13; Nagaraj 2025 (diagnostic use only).

**G13. Null-change control** (F6 M1/M5). A pair of acquisitions between which no real change is possible (a repeat delivery of the same flight, or two acquisitions weeks apart) must produce zero change after the heal; report the count of changes emitted. Zero labels. Source: Schmidt 2019 repositioning series (with the caveat that its zero came from the size floor); Reuter 2012's design principle (share initialisation, never conclusions).

**G14. Labelling protocol for per-year presence** (F6 M7, narrowed). Label per-year presence *without the neighbouring years' model output visible*; read *change* as a paired interval (a blinded per-chip gold cannot hold a change label, and derived change needs per-chip error < 1.8% to beat the 42 real losses); never fold the temporal number into the accuracy number; expect reader-specific, one-sided disagreement (Carass: lesion FPR 0.138 vs 0.563 depending on which rater is truth); refuse any consensus built from the methods being scored. Source: Carass 2017 (blinding sentence verbatim; the rest is the skeptic's narrowing).

**G15. Registration tolerances** (F2 M3, F1 M6, F10 M4). A 255-band of width p95 inter-year offset (in target-year pixels) around every 0/1 transition, inside which the healer may write only 255; cross-year comparison with a tolerance no tighter than p95; the collar's width from `phase4/qc/coregistration.csv`, not from a 0.5 s audio collar. Zero labels.

**G16. Secondary measurement reads.** Stratified AssPr on the 42-loss population (HOTA Eq. 26: AssPr_α = (1/|TP|)Σ|TPA(c)|/(|TPA(c)|+|FPA(c)|)) — catches the degenerate heal-everything run at 0.5 where AssA/HOTA score 0.99; a MOSEv2-style split (must-not-fill on losses; should-fill on verified flickers, excluding crown-years adjacent to 2020). Both re-partition existing labels. The critic's fetched alternative to AssPr is the AOGM/TRA edit-operation count (Matula et al. 2015, PLOS ONE; §4.1 M5): every spurious temporal link costs w_ED once, so the heal-everything run cannot be diluted by the 1,170 clean crowns; transposition to crown-years is inferred and was not adjudicated by a skeptic. **G17. Zero-label identifiability and degeneracy checks** (F8 M4, M7; F4 M6). Data cloning per stratum (k·Var(posterior) → 0 or the miss/absent split is not recoverable there); boundary refusal (a stratum whose fit returns p̂ = 1 or ψ̂ = 1 is the "everything is a miss" attractor); the two-fit check (miss curve fixed at panel values vs free under a flat prior — if the free fit does not converge to the panel, every correction rides on the prior). Sources: Lele 2012 p.24; Welsh 2013 p.6; Klausch 2026 §7.

## 7. Three to test first; one to refuse

A "test first" had to (a) survive the skeptics, (b) cost at most one labelling session, and (c) attack one of the three things the review found missing — γ_conditional, the 42-loss ceiling, or the both-sides rule's evidence base.

**1. The fill-side laundering audit with a null-change control (G1 + G13; attacks the ceiling).** Sample ~300 crown-years the heal changed from its own output; adjudicate each as a paired interval read; check the adjudicator against a lidar epoch or finer imagery on a sub-sample (otherwise the bound is on human–model agreement, not laundering); publish k/300 with its exact bound beside 0/42, 6.9%, and n_eligible. Add the null-change pair. Add stratified AssPr on the losses as a secondary read. Why first: it is the only route to a laundering bound below 1% at this budget; it cannot be gamed by panel avoidance; it fits one session; and it tests the heal that exists rather than a design. What it cannot do: tighten the loss-side bound, which is fixed by the 42.

**2. The gap-length spectrum, re-specified and mutation-tested (G3 + G4 + G5; attacks the both-sides rule's evidence base; zero labels).** Publish (L_years, H(L), laundered(L), triples(L), n_eligible, crowns_deleted) for the closing baseline and for the tiered heal; report n_eligible first — if it is zero the panel cannot see any laundering by any gap-bridging rule and that fact should be stated before any spectrum is read; seed a known-bad L and show laundered(·) fires; run the temporal median as the negative control. Why second: it costs nothing, it is the comparator the brief asked field 9 for, and it converts the BLIND-tier gap length from a rule into a curve with a stated power.

**3. Measure γ_conditional from the impossible triples, and α from the 42 (G7 + G10; attacks γ).** The 42 verified losses cannot yield γ_conditional: they are endpoint-verified absent at 2024, so any crown removed and regrown within the interval reads present–present and sits among the no-change points — conditioning on the 42 conditions on no regain (the critic's correction; this report's first draft proposed exactly that error). The population that can contain a loss-then-regain is the impossible triples and interior absent runs at the verified no-change points: adjudicate each interior absence as detection failure (a tree the model missed) or real absence (bare ground, stump, a replant), which splits the triples the kill criterion already counts into flicker and loss-then-regain and gives P(regain within Δt | removal) directly. The 42 then supply what they can: α = P(a removed footprint reads absent at ≥ 0.5) and the post-loss false-canopy / ingrowth rate, from reading the 2017–2023 acquisitions inside the fixed footprints (α ≈ 1 is currently assumed; 42 events bound it to roughly ±0.06–0.09). Why third: every probabilistic mechanism in four fields stalls on γ_conditional; the panel's 2 gains measure colonisation of empty sites, the wrong quantity; the labels are interior reads of an existing panel. If γ_conditional lands near 0.2, the posterior gate becomes usable at the fine-year/coarse-year split the project's tiers already encode; if it lands near 10⁻³, the hard gates are doing work no posterior can and should be kept.

**Refuse, until γ_conditional has been measured: any posterior-as-licence — PMBM existence, dynamic-occupancy posterior, SAR-EM imputation, and their HMM equivalents with a fitted transition matrix.** The refusal is conditional on test 3's outcome and should be re-read when it lands; what is unconditional is that no posterior may license a fill before that number exists.
