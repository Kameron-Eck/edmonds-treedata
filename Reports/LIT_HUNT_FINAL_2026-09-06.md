### 3a. PACC — Panel-Anchored Cross-Date Certification (designer's one-line thesis: "the paired human panel is the estimator, not the validator")
1. Invariant reference frame. Anchor lattice on the 2 m analysis grid: A_can = lidar canopy in both 2005 and 2016 (24.90 % of the city; ≥4 m interior core 5.04 %, 310,294 cells), A_bare = lidar non-canopy in both epochs and impervious (eroded, 5.52 %, 340,001 cells). Nothing is resampled; nearest-neighbour warps onto the grid.
2. Distance stratification, the load-bearing move: bands 0–2, 2–4, 4–8, 8–16, >16 m from the lidar canopy boundary inside each anchor. Nuth & Kääb's stable-terrain bias as a function of covariates, not a scalar.
3. Per-delivery response profile R_d = P(map = canopy | band) at the deployed cut, for each of the 37 deliveries on its own footprint. No DN and no radiometric quantity crosses a delivery boundary, which is the escape F4 requires.
4. In-bracket invariance fit on the five epochs whose stability is lidar-certified at both ends (2009–2016), then extrapolation, with a contamination term ℓ_t (share of anchor cells that really changed after 2016) estimated from a disjoint simple random sample of anchor cells (Card 1982 form R_obs = (1 − ℓ)R_true + ℓ·R_bare).
5. Estimation: the fixed paired human panel at every epoch is the estimator; the map enters only as a locator for stratification and as a GREG auxiliary with an estimated coefficient allowed to collapse to zero (McRoberts 2014; Sannier et al. 2014; Stehman 2013; Olofsson 2014).
6. Certification, pre-registered with a commit hash before labelling: (a) |Δ_map,corrected − Δ_panel| > 1.96·SE_panel on ≥2 of 3 intervals, evaluated at the measured interpreter error; (b) the domain statistic B = Σ_h W_h [P(map_t2 = canopy) − P(map_t1 = canopy)]_h on human-certified stable points exceeds 1.0 pp with a CI excluding zero, or McNemar χ² > 3.84 on the discordant pairs. If either fires the map is demoted to locator-only and the panel carries the estimate alone. A fourth test, a flagged-loss review lane with a commission threshold (Pierce/WDFW HRCD precedent), is specified and was not run.
Scaling (measured rates, not assumed): 1.94 s per paired label (1,393 Panel A labels, phase4/qc/panel_a_labels.csv), elapsed/worked 2.16; 77.9 GPU-min per delivery (Scripts/run_registry.csv). Certification to ±1.0 pp needs 4,657 stable points ≈ 2.5 h of labelling; ±1.5 pp needs 2,070 ≈ 1.1 h. No training, no new inference.

### 3d. Why B is not claimed, and what would make it so
B passes the checklist's letter (PACC 17/17/16, item 7 unanimous, precedents on disk for every load-bearing component) and fails the contract's purpose: the map series does not carry the trend in the one measurement designed to let it (GREG gain ≈ 4 %), the standalone anchor correction is killed at the present sample size, and the certification statistic is undetermined with an unpropagated arbiter error of the same order as the effect. The design is nevertheless implementable in a day of compute and about three hours of blinded labelling, and its kill test can fail on the failure mode that matters. The single experiment that closes it, recommended by the round-5 critic and by two PACC judges independently: a pre-registered, date-blinded, order-randomized re-interpretation of a Neyman-sized stable stratum (N ≈ 2,070 for ±1.5 pp, 4,657 for ±1.0 pp), with same-window replicate deliveries seeded as true nulls and the fail threshold fixed in advance. It supplies PACC's missing term, de-circularizes the Census's kill test 3, and tests E2-MRF's edge claim if the anchors include 0–2 m cells. The parallel session's fit-free step-shape certifier (Olofsson-weighted precision 0.249 → 0.431, empirical null from the same-flight twin and hard-negative parcels, tiered output with REVIEW-TERMINAL for the 31 % of loss that eight epochs cannot corroborate) is the natural locator for PACC's review lane and is, by its authors' own note, unreproducible from repo plus lake until it is ported as an instrument.
