THIS OVERTURNS THE REPORT'S CORE "NO CITY-FUNDED LIDAR" CONCLUSION. Summary (full detail in the
report body and in ila-3106507.txt):
  - City of Edmonds signed an Intergovernmental Services Agreement with Snohomish County,
    executed ~7/16-7/20/2026, to procure high-definition LiDAR data for the incorporated area of
    Edmonds (8.92 square miles), riding a Snohomish County Cost Sharing Agreement with WA DNR
    (DNR Contract No. 93-111079, $304,606 total) under DNR's existing lidar services contract
    with NV5 Geospatial, Inc. (DNR Contract #93-102831).
  - Edmonds' share: $3,300, capped, invoiced by the County on delivery.
  - Funding chain: WA Dept of Commerce Salmon Recovery Through Local Planning Grant Agreement
    No. 26-63117-011 (Snohomish County Council Motion No. 26-104) funds the County's side.
  - NV5 Geospatial proposal ("Snohomish County Leaf-on Lidar Mapping Services, Washington",
    dated April 15, 2026, under DNR lidar RFP No. 22-03 — the SAME master RFP number behind the
    2022 WA_LidarGaps_C22 project already in the report's §2/§10.3) covers 823 square miles
    across unincorporated Snohomish County and 11 participating cities (Stanwood, Marysville,
    Arlington, Granite Falls, Mukilteo, Everett, Snohomish, Lake Stevens, Brier, Bothell,
    Edmonds).
  - Collection window per NV5's proposal: "leaf on conditions in mid-June to July 2026" —
    scheduled, matching the staff email's "summer 2026 fly-overs" almost verbatim. NOTE: this is
    the vendor's proposed window, not confirmed completion — Exhibit B was countersigned by DNR
    on 2026-07-22 (Teigen, Thomas signature block), i.e. after the proposal's stated collection
    window, and the cost-share's own "period of performance" runs "July 17, 2026, or the date of
    execution, whichever is later, to January 31, 2027." Whether NV5 flew ahead of the paperwork
    (common in practice) or collection slipped past the stated window cannot be determined from
    these records alone. This bears directly on the "next year or 2028" hedge in the staff email.
  - Deliverables: full QL1 lidar spec — classified point cloud (LAS 1.4), hydro-flattened bare-
    earth DEM, DSM, breaklines, intensity imagery, FGDC metadata — at >=8 pulses/m2, <=10cm
    vertical accuracy (RMSEz).
  - Products delivered "90 business days from the date of successful acquisition and receipt of
    survey" — i.e., roughly Q4 2026 if collection completes on schedule, consistent with the
    staff email's "to be completed next year [2027] or in 2028, depending on budget outcomes"
    given County obligations after 12/31/2026 are contingent on appropriation (Sec. 2 of the
    ILA), and the Agreement's own title bracket is "From Execution to July 2027".
