## TL;DR — what you're inheriting

The **grass-vs-tree fix is half-landed**. The new 4th input channel — a REAL canopy
height model (`lidar_snoh_chm.tif`, USGS 3DEP height-above-ground in metres) — is
built, correct, and visually excellent. But the first training run on it (2016)
**COLLAPSED**: `val_iou` climbed to 0.515 by epoch 5, then fell off a cliff to ~0.01
and never recovered. The saved model is the undertrained epoch-5 checkpoint. Result:
a big regression vs the RGB baseline and no grass improvement.

**Your job:** stabilise + rebalance the training so the model can actually exploit
the height channel, then re-measure the grass fix. The CHM raster is NOT the problem —
do not rebuild it. The problem is the training dynamics introduced by the v030 changes.

## Diagnosis — ranked hypotheses

**The architecture is unchanged from the stable v029 struct run** (same trainable
inflated input-conv, same LR schedule). Only two things changed: (a) the 4th channel
(weak `struct` texture → strong `chm` height), and (b) the negative rebalancing. So:

1. **[MOST LIKELY] Class-balance overshoot → all-background collapse.** v030 pushed
   background hard: `HARD_NEG_FRACTION` 0.0→0.30, `BACKGROUND_BUDGET_FRACTION` 0.20→0.30
   (actual 36%), +92 green hard-negs, +74 negative-site tiles — AND `pos_weight` raw
   2.774 clamped to 1.3 starves the canopy class. Net: canopy is under-weighted from
   both the sampler and the loss → optimiser falls into the all-background basin
   (val_iou→0.01). This is the single biggest delta from the run that DIDN'T collapse.
2. **[COMPOUNDING] Strong CHM channel + trainable inflated input-conv at LR 5e-5.**
   `chm` carries far stronger, structured signal than `struct`. With the frozen
   encoder expecting RGB-like activations, an aggressive input-conv update on the
   height channel can push the encoder out of distribution. Struct was too weak to do
   this; chm may not be.
3. **[SECONDARY] LR / schedule / single-seed variance.** LR_PHASE_A=5e-5 may be too hot
   for the new dynamics; Phase B never recovered from the Phase-A collapse.

**Cleanest disambiguating experiment:** re-run 2016 **RGB-only under the v030 sampling**
(`--no-hillshade`, same new HARD_NEG/BG constants). If RGB *also* collapses → it's the
class balance (#1). If only chm collapses → it's the channel/input-conv (#2).

## Suggested experiment order (cheap → expensive)

1. **Disambiguate** (1 short train): 2016 `--no-hillshade` under current v030 constants.
   Collapse? → it's class balance. Stable? → it's the channel.
2. **Rebalance** (most likely fix): dial `HARD_NEG_FRACTION`→0.15, `BACKGROUND_BUDGET_FRACTION`
   →0.22, and EITHER raise `COARSE_POS_WEIGHT_MAX`→~2.5 OR set `COARSE_USE_POS_WEIGHT=False`.
   Retrain 2016 `--hs-source chm`. Target: val_iou holds/climbs past 0.55, Phase B improves,
   test recall back toward ~0.85+.
3. **If still unstable → channel/optics:** lower `LR_PHASE_A`→2e-5, and/or give the
   inflated input-conv its own smaller LR / longer freeze / brief warm-up.
4. Once stable + IoU ≳ RGB baseline (0.72): re-run `phase4_viz.py --year 2016`; the win
   condition is **grass-FP rate well below the RGB/struct rows with recall held**.
5. Then **2000** (temporal-drift year) to see how the ~2016 height prior degrades.

Do NOT re-tile between train attempts unless you change sampling constants (tiling is
where HARD_NEG/BG take effect — those DO require a re-tile). LR/pos_weight changes are
train-only. Re-tile ≈ 20 min; train ≈ 10–20 min on A100.

## Interpretation caveats (do not over-trust any number)

- **Label ceiling / circularity:** coarse-year "truth" = the 2020 canopy mask reprojected
  onto the year grid (`canopy_label_from_2020_mask`). Grass is background only if it was
  in 2020; forest "grass-FP" partly counts 2020-vs-2016 label disagreement, not real lawn
  errors. Trust lawn/field tiles + the committed negative sites over forest tiles.
- **Honest validation set unbuilt.** Random-point photo-interpretation (incl. grass) is
  the real DG2 gate and does not exist yet. Until it does, IoU/AUROC are measured against
  imperfect labels. This is the eventual foundational task (out of scope for the collapse fix).
- 2016 is the best case (on-year lidar, full coverage). Early years (2000–2012) will see
  the ~2016 height prior drift; `HS_DROPOUT` + the RGB pathway are the mitigations.
