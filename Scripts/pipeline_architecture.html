Edmonds Temporal Canopy Pipeline · architecture reference

What goes in, what runs, what comes out

Four production phases turn 18 aerial acquisitions into per-year canopy masks. A separate measurement layer scores them. The one structural feature worth knowing before you change anything: Phase 3's output is Phase 4's training label, so a blind spot in the first becomes a blind spot in every year that follows.

The loop that shapes everything

Phase 3 predicts the 2020 canopy mask. Phase 4 then uses that mask as ground-truth labels for every coarse year. In code: MASK_2020 = P3_DIR / "edmonds_canopy_mask_2020.tif" feeding canopy_label_from_2020_mask().

Hand labels exist for five conifer sites, all mature stands. So whatever the 2020 model fails to see, the mask omits; whatever the mask omits, no later year is ever taught. The blind spot doesn't just persist across years — it is actively re-taught to each one. This is why measured accuracy barely improves when the model architecture does.

Measurement layer

A separate subsystem. It reads outputs and references; it never writes a training label. That boundary is what keeps evaluation honest, and it is enforced by convention rather than by code — worth remembering before wiring anything new.

Scoring

  • phase4_qc_indep.py — recall / precision against C-CAP
  • phase4_qc_score.py — against the NDVI+CHM reference
  • phase4_qc_inventory.py — catches empty or partial rasters before they are scored

Diagnosis

  • phase4_ref_agreement.py — splits the miss into real vs unmeasurable
  • phase4_qc_forest_misses.py — why canopy is missed, by height and spectrum
  • phase4_qc_dashboard.py — every live number on one page

Where the evidence points

Not architecture. Three models with every structural advantage (4-channel RGB+CHM, NIR, out-of-sample AUROC 0.953–0.954) score honest recall 0.65–0.66 — inside the same band as far weaker years. Better models do not close the gap.

Partly measurement. The two references contradict each other on 15–17% of pixels. Scored only where they agree, recall is 0.73–0.76 and precision 0.96–0.97. About a third of the apparent miss cannot be attributed to the model at all.

Mostly label distribution. Across five years, three sensors and two recipes, recalled canopy averages ~24 m and missed canopy ~12 m — every time. The model learned tall = canopy, because that is what the labels contain. The fix is targeted labels at 5–15 m and deciduous canopy, not more data of the same kind.
