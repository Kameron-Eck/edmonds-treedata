RULES (all require >=3 consecutive hourly observations, gaps >95 min break the run):
  R1_primary  no BKN/OVC/VV layer with base <= 8000 ft; vis >= 7 sm; no precip
              wxcode; >=1 hour of the run with solar elevation > 20 deg.
  R2_maxelim  as R1 but ANY BKN/OVC/VV blocks regardless of height; sun floor 15 deg.
  R3_lowcloud as R1 but cloud cutoff 5000 ft.
  SPEC_asasked the criterion in the task brief: EVERY hour of the run must have
              solar elevation > 30 deg; cloud cutoff 8000 ft. -> FALSIFIED, see below.
  Precip tokens treated as blocking: TS RA SN DZ PL GR GS SG IC UP FZ.
  Missing visibility (0.06% of observations) treated as non-blocking.

BOUNDARY SENSITIVITY (measured): the SPEC criterion's cloud test at exactly 8000 ft is
knife-edge. "base <= 8000 blocks" -> 38/46 control; "base < 8000 blocks" -> 40/46
(2017-05-08 and 2023-04-20 flip to PASS). The named 2017 control therefore goes 4/5 or
5/5 depending on an inclusive-vs-exclusive comparison. The falsification of the 30 deg
sun floor does NOT depend on any boundary: 2015-02-15 was CLR with vis 10 sm for all 8
daylight hours, was flown, and has a max solar elevation of 29.30 deg.
Irreducible false negatives (fail every rule at all available stations): 2015-02-18
(OVC 2400-5000 ft at KPAE, KSEA and KBFI; 5 frames flown) and 2023-05-07 (BKN 900-2000
ft at KPAE; 34 frames flown).

PER-HOUR CONTROL (the only one available): the 2012 photo-centre index publishes flight
TIMES. 2012-03-23 13:25-13:44 falls inside the passing clear/sun>30 hours 10:53-15:53;
2012-04-07 12:08-12:46 falls inside 09:53-15:53. Both held.

FALSE-NEGATIVE BOUND: 0 failures in n=46 -> one-sided 95% upper bound on the per-day
false-negative rate = 6.3% (rule of three 6.5%). An ELIMINATED day is unlikely, not
impossible.
