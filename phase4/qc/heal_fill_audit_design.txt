-- OLOFSSON WEIGHTS AND THE ESTIMATOR -----------------------------
  Population = the healer's own fill components. W_h = N_h / N over fills.
  Weighted laundering rate:      lambda_hat = sum_h W_h * (k_h / n_h)
  Variance (the convention of phase4_qc_design_power.py::estimate — within-stratum
  Var(p_h) = p_h(1-p_h)/(n_h - 1), a single binary outcome being the two-cell case
  of its multinomial covariance):
        Var(lambda_hat) = sum_h W_h^2 * p_h(1-p_h)/(n_h - 1)
        95% half-width  = 1.959963985 * sqrt(Var)

    assumed p   half-width  (weighted, at the realised allocation)

        0.010       0.0122
        0.020       0.0172
        0.050       0.0267
        0.100       0.0368

-- DETECTABLE EFFECT ----------------------------------------------
  The endpoint bound this replaces, READ from heal_vs_gold.csv: 0 of 42
  verified losses laundered -> exact one-sided 95% upper bound
  1 - 0.05^(1/42) = 0.0688 (6.88%). Those 42 are spent: no draw
  on the panel can tighten it, which is why this audit samples the fills instead.

  At n = 300 fills, k laundered, exact one-sided 95% upper bound (Clopper-Pearson;
  k = 0 is G1's closed form 1 - 0.05^(1/n)):
     k      k/n   upper bound   verdict vs the 6.9% endpoint bound
     0   0.0000        0.0099   tighter
     1   0.0033        0.0157   tighter
     2   0.0067        0.0208   tighter

     5   0.0167        0.0347   tighter
    10   0.0333        0.0559   tighter
    20   0.0667        0.0954   NO IMPROVEMENT

  So the audit beats the endpoint bound for any k <= 13 of 300, and at k = 0 it
  reports < 0.99% — a 6.9x tightening.
  G1's teeth, quoted from Reports/LIT_HEALING_ANALOGUES_2026-09-08.md §7: the base
  loss rate is 3.47%, so a fill-side rate below 1% requires the heal to fill
  real-loss cells at under 29% of the rate it fills random cells.

  THE SRS CAVEAT, stated because it is easy to miss: 1 - 0.05^(1/n) is a BINOMIAL
  bound and this draw is stratified with deliberate 2x oversampling. It applies
  exactly to the UNWEIGHTED sample rate. The city-level weighted rate carries the
  stratified variance above instead, and the two answer different questions.

-- THE READER'S OWN ERROR RATE ------------------------------------
  Control draw: 300 units, matched stratum-for-stratum, from absences with
  EXACTLY ONE aligned flank present — the nearest untouched population at the same
  scale (see CADENCE above for why the literal both-flanks control is empty).
  Let eps = the rate at which the reader calls canopy ABSENT where it was present.
  Rogan-Gladen: lambda_hat = (p_obs - eps) / (1 - beta - eps), beta the miss rate;
  SE(eps) = sqrt(eps(1-eps)/m), and a lambda under Z*SE(eps) is UNDETERMINED, not
  zero (CLAUDE.md 3.5).

  WHICH m, and this is the binding fact of the whole design: eps is only knowable
  where truth is known INDEPENDENTLY of the reader, i.e. on the ADJUDICATED rows —
  not on the 300-unit control draw as a whole. m is bracketed, not fixed:
    lower  m = 55   adjudicated CONTROLS alone
    upper  m = 120   every adjudicated row, fills included — an adjudicated fill
                whose lidar reads PRESENT is as valid an eps observation as a
                control is, under the double-sampling reading
  Which of the two applies is settled only once the lidar is read, because only
  then is it known how many adjudicated rows carry a PRESENT truth at all.
   assumed eps    SE at m=55    floor   SE at m=120    floor   (floor = Z*SE)
          0.02        0.0189   0.0370        0.0128   0.0250
          0.05        0.0294   0.0576        0.0199   0.0390
          0.10        0.0405   0.0793        0.0274   0.0537

  THE ASYMMETRY THIS EXPOSES, and it is the most decision-relevant line in the note:
  n = 300 fills buys a k/n bound near 1.0%, while the floor under a
  resolvable lambda sits several times higher. The bound and the floor are bought by
  DIFFERENT numbers — the 300 fills and the adjudicated rows — and enlarging one
  without the other moves nothing. ADJUDICATE_SHARE is a chosen constant, not a
  sourced one: G1 says 'a sub-sample' and names no size. Raising it is Kam's call.

  ASSUMED, and not testable inside this design: that the reader errs at the same
  rate on fills and on controls. The control's flanks differ, so a reader can tell
  the two apart — it is a DISCRIMINATION control (does the both-sides rule buy
  anything in the reader's eyes), not a blinded error-rate control. blind_order
  randomises presentation order only; it cannot blind the flanks.

-- ADJUDICATOR SUB-SAMPLE -----------------------------------------
  adjudicable rows: 220 of 600; flagged for adjudication: 120  (20% target, stratified by tier and is_control)

  reader the answer.

-- WHAT IS MEASURED, DERIVED, ASSUMED -----------------------------
  MEASURED: every population and cell count above, from the healer's own output on
            the 8-epoch stack; the untouched-two-sided count; lidar coverage.
  DERIVED : the allocation, the weights, the bounds and half-widths — arithmetic on
            those counts under the stated formulas.
  ASSUMED : that reader error is equal on fills and controls; that a component's
            equivalent-area diameter is comparable to the crown polygons' diameter_m
            (same estimator, quantised to the 2 m lattice); that units are
            independent — they are not strictly, since one crown can be a fill in
            two epochs, which makes the binomial bound mildly optimistic.
  NOT MEASURED HERE: whether any fill is laundering. This instrument draws the
            sample and states the design; the human reads it.
